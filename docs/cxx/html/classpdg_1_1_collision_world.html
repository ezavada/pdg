Detailed Description

Solver-independent collision traversal. Worlds borrow registered owners, and retain colliders while dispatching. No Sprite or rendering dependency.

Detect/resolve a collected world frame, then deliver begin/stay/end contacts.

Detect and resolve a collected collider frame, then deliver contact phases.

SpriteLayer performs this automatically for its owners. A custom native loop must advance its bodies before passing the current set of attached colliders. Inputs are borrowed; this world retains the handles used by its current and previous contact frames. Omitted or disabled colliders end their previous contacts. Duplicate pointers are ignored. Filtering combines collider categories, masks, groups, body relationships, layer permissions and the optional additional filter. Basic response handles pairs not registered together in the native physics space. Call this after the native solve when using Chipmunk; it does not advance that space. Native pairs reuse the solver contact manifold, including sensors and sleeping contacts, without repeating geometric detection. Static/static, mixed-solver and zero-time queries retain geometric detection. Sleeping contacts continue to report Stay with zero new impulse.

Parameters
collidersCurrent attached colliders to inspect.
secondsFinite nonnegative step duration in seconds. Zero detects contacts without applying basic response impulses.
pairFilterOptional additional pair predicate. False suppresses that pair. Leave empty for ordinary collider filtering. The predicate must not mutate the world; use contact listeners for changes after solving.
