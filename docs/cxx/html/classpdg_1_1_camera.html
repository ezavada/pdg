Camera Class Reference
Animation | Graphics

Animated view owned by a Port or shared by layers.Each Port owns its camera. A layer uses its retained explicit camera, otherwise its port's built-in camera. Layer cameras own their destination viewport. Location plus center offset maps to the Port anchor or layer viewport center. Rotation is the view's world orientation; zoom and scale magnify the view. Size is animation metadata and does not resize the destination viewport. More...

Detailed Description

Animated view owned by a Port or shared by layers.

Each Port owns its camera. A layer uses its retained explicit camera, otherwise its port's built-in camera. Layer cameras own their destination viewport. Location plus center offset maps to the Port anchor or layer viewport center. Rotation is the view's world orientation; zoom and scale magnify the view. Size is animation metadata and does not resize the destination viewport.

Animated view shared by ports and layers.

Scene-owned cameras advance on their scene clock. Layer overrides remain available; Scene describes ownership and pause behavior.

See Camera visibility, fades and transitions for camera visibility, composed opacity, fades and crossfades.

Camera also inherits EventEmitter. Subscribe on the camera, rather than on a layer, to receive eventType_ZoomComplete with CameraZoomInfo {camera, zoom}. zoomTo() targets an absolute magnification; zoom() multiplies the starting zoom. Queued factors resolve when the operation starts. Both emit once per completed operation, including a zero-duration operation (synchronously unless queued or delayed). Cancelled/replaced operations and setZoom() do not emit. Pausing the schedule delays completion. Shared attachments do not duplicate notifications.

const auto& completion = *static_cast<pdg::CameraZoomInfo*>(data);
// completion.camera identifies the camera; completion.zoom is its final zoom.
return false;
}
};

ZoomListener listener;
pdg::Camera camera;
camera.addHandler(&listener, pdg::eventType_ZoomComplete);
camera.zoomTo(2, 0.5).andThen().zoom(0.5, 0.5);
// Detached cameras are advanced manually; attached cameras use the runtime clock.
camera.animate(1);
camera.removeHandler(&listener, pdg::eventType_ZoomComplete);

◆ matchCutTo()

Camera & matchCutTo ( Camera & destination,
const CameraMatchOptions & options )

Align Sprite compositions and carry apparent motion across a hard cut.

Align Sprite centers and optionally projected bounds before a hard cut. Matching uses a shared Port/viewport. The source schedule owns both phases. settleReturnsCamera restores the incoming base/follow view; false retains framing.

The source camera's shared schedule owns approachSeconds and settleSeconds. At cut time, Sprite projected bounds centers and screen velocity match. The CameraMatchMode in options.mode names the reference shot and optional size matching:

Mode Behavior
matchSource (default) Preserve the outgoing shot and align the incoming view to its live framing and apparent motion.
matchSourceAndSize Match the source shot, including projected bounds size and its rate of change using zoom.
matchTarget Ease the outgoing shot toward the target's live framing.
matchTargetAndSize Approach the target shot's framing and size, carrying apparent motion and size rate through the cut.

matchSource and matchTarget leave zoom unchanged. Both cameras remain reserved through settling; andThen() follows settling. Visibility and picking switch at the approach boundary. Pause and cancellation use the ordinary source schedule. Before-cut cancellation restores visibility and removes corrections; after-cut cancellation retains the incoming framing. Checked Sprite lifetimes, reassignment and attachment removal cancel safely. Pending matches cannot serialize, repeat, reverse or restart.

Parameters
destinationRetained incoming camera sharing the subjects' Port and viewport.
optionsCameraMatchOptions with required distinct matchSource/matchTarget Sprite pointers.
Returns
This source camera for chaining.

CameraMatchOptions defaults to approachSeconds=0.4, settleSeconds=0.4, mode=matchSource, settleReturnsCamera=false, approachEasing=easeInQuad and settleEasing=easeOutQuad. Phase durations must be finite and positive; easings must be monotonic with zero/one endpoints. settleReturnsCamera=false retains matched position/zoom and deceleration travel. True removes corrections toward the destination's live base/following view. Following remains enabled, and retained framing updates its follow offset. Matching uses a separate adjustment from ordinary effects and base animation. getLocation()/getZoom() expose the base pose during matching; layer coordinate conversions include temporary matching adjustments.

Note
