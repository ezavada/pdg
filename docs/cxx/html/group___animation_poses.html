Detailed Description

Rig structure, owned pose snapshots, coordinate spaces, and authored metadata.

pdg::AnimationRig stores immutable bones, sockets, and bindings. A pdg::AnimationPose owns its mutable transforms and shares the rig; copying a pose does not share mutable transforms. pdg::AnimationTransform describes translation, rotation, scale, and opacity. pdg::AnimationSpace selects local, rig, or world coordinates; world coordinates belong to the Sprite's owning layer.

pdg::AnimationMetadata retains authored variables and tags. Bone, socket, and binding IDs are scoped to their rig instance and revision.
