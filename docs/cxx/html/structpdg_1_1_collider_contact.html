Detailed Description

One contact, oriented from collider to other. Coordinates use the owning world. Shape IDs remain stable until removed. Sensors, sleeping contacts and End contacts have zero impulse. Chipmunk contacts reuse its solved manifold; point is the mean contact midpoint and penetration is its greatest overlap depth. Collider pointers are borrowed for the duration of contact dispatch.
